# python-ai-sprint

An 8-week sprint, from Nov 4 to Dec 31 2026, to go from Python to shipping AI projects.

| Step | Dates | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Nov 4 to 17 | Python, NumPy, Pandas, charts |
| 2 | Nov 18 to Dec 1 | Classical ML (scikit-learn) |
| 3 | Dec 2 to 15 | Deep learning (PyTorch) |
| 4 | Dec 16 to 31 | Capstone: LLM / RAG app |

Rules: no zero days, 70% building, and a commit every day.
